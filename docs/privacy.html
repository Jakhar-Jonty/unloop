Privacy Policy

Unloop · effective 22 October 2026

The short version: Unloop does not collect, send or sell any of your data. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything it knows stays on your phone.

Who we are

Unloop is an Android home screen (launcher) that helps you use your phone more intentionally. Contact: jontysingh055@gmail.com.

What Unloop uses, and why

Unloop reads the following on your device only, to make its features work. None of it ever leaves your phone, because the app cannot connect to the internet.

DataWhy
List of installed appsTo show your home screen and app list, and to let you choose apps to pause, limit or hide.
App usage statistics (with your permission)To show today's screen time, your weekly reflection, app time limits and the plant.
Which app is currently open, via the Accessibility service (with your permission)For "Pause everywhere": to show the pause or time-limit screen when a paused or limited app is opened from a notification, Recents or a link; to lock the screen when you double-tap home; and to open the notification shade when you swipe down. It does not read screen content, messages, passwords or anything you type.
Do Not Disturb access (optional)To silence notifications during Focus mode and schedules, and restore your setting afterwards.
Calendar (optional)To show your next event on the home screen.
Your tasks, notes and settingsStored in the app's private storage so the app works.

Accessibility service

Unloop's Accessibility service only listens for which app moves to the front of the screen. It is used solely for the features above, which help you limit distracting apps. The information is used in the moment and is not stored, logged, transmitted or shared. You can turn the service off at any time in your phone's Settings → Accessibility.

Sharing

Unloop does not share data with anyone. If you use "Share my week", Unloop opens Android's share sheet with a short text summary, and you choose where (if anywhere) to send it.

Storage, backup and deletion

Data is stored in Unloop's private app storage on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup under Google's backup terms. Uninstalling Unloop, or clearing its storage in Settings → Apps → Unloop, deletes all of it.

Children

Unloop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
